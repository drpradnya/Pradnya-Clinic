Questions

Before you come in.

Why homeopathy?

Homeopathy looks at the whole person, not only the diagnosis. Remedies are chosen using your symptoms together with sleep, appetite, stress and temperament. Many people choose it because they want an individual, unhurried consultation and a gentle approach, often alongside their regular care. It is a personal choice, best made with a qualified doctor.

Who developed homeopathy?

Homeopathy was developed by Dr. Samuel Hahnemann (1755 to 1843), a German physician. He set out its principles in the 1790s and published them in The Organon of Medicine, first issued in 1810. It spread across Europe, reached India in the 19th century, and is today one of the AYUSH systems of medicine in India.

Common misconceptions about homeopathy
  • "It is the same as herbal medicine." It is not. Homeopathic remedies can come from plants, minerals and other sources, and are prepared by a specific method of serial dilution.
  • "It only works slowly, or only for minor problems." The pace and the benefit depend on the person and the condition. Dr. Pradnya will tell you honestly what to expect, and when you need other medical care.
  • "It replaces all other medicines." No. It can be used alongside conventional care and should never replace urgent treatment. Do not stop a prescribed medicine without medical advice.
  • "It is only a placebo." This is debated. Scientific studies have not all agreed, and major reviews have found limited evidence for some conditions. Many patients value the individual attention and report benefit. Ask questions during your consultation and expect straight answers.
  • "Natural means risk-free." Any treatment is best taken under a qualified doctor's guidance, with a full history of what else you take.
Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are best, so you are not kept waiting. Use the form above or message the clinic on WhatsApp.

Should I bring my reports and medicines?

Yes. Bring any recent reports, prescriptions and a list of what you currently take, so the doctor has the full picture.

Can I stop my current medicines?

No. Never stop or change a prescribed medicine without talking to the doctor who prescribed it. Tell Dr. Gawande about everything you take.

Is homeopathy right for emergencies?

No. For severe pain, breathing trouble, heavy bleeding, chest pain or any emergency, go to the nearest hospital or call emergency services.
